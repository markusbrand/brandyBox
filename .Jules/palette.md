## 2023-10-06 - Embedded Call-to-Actions in Empty States
**Learning:** Empty states without immediate, in-context call-to-actions require users to hunt for the primary actions (like upload or create folder) elsewhere in the UI, increasing cognitive load and slowing down onboarding.
**Action:** Always embed primary and secondary actions (e.g., Upload, New Folder) directly into the empty state component to seamlessly guide the user's next steps.
